![Northeast IPM Center logo](/images/news/ipm-online-conference.png#right) Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is an integral part of the Climate Smart Farming mission, and the CSF Program collaborates with the New York State IPM Center and Northeast IPM Center.

The Northeast IPM Center hosted its second annual IPM Online Conference in 2016, which featured IPM updates in rapid-style 5-minute presentations. Speakers discussed one or two highlights from their projects, covering IPM-related research, education, and extension currently taking place in and around the Northeast.

Presentations from the conference are available on the Northeast IPM Center's website and YouTube channel.
