![NYS Climate Impacts Assessment](/images/news/nys-impact.png#left) Two members of Cornell's Climate Smart Farming Team, Dr. Deborah Aller and Dr. Allison Chatrchyan, were selected to lead the Agriculture Chapter of the Climate Impacts Assessment project for New York State.

To explore how the warming environment will affect New York's communities, ecosystems, and economy, Gov. Kathy Hochul named several Cornellians to the state's Climate Impacts Assessment project. The group conducted research and suggested how the state can best prepare for climate change and adapt for the future.

The research effort was led by the New York State Energy Research and Development Authority (NYSERDA) in partnership with many other groups, including a large contingent of Cornell scientists and Cornell Cooperative Extension educators. The assessment was completed in early 2023.
