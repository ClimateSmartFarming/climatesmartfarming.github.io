The annual Cornell Research Field Day will be held **August 1st** at Musgrave Research Farm in Aurora, NY from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. The event will include walking and hay wagon tours with multiple tour stops and speakers. The program is free and open to the public. A light breakfast and lunch will also be provided.

## Tour Stops and Speakers

- **Vipan Kumar** — Herbicide programs for weed control in corn and soybean
- **Mike Hunter & Lynn Sosnoskie** — Weed seed movement and the importance of cleaning equipment
- **Louis Longchamps** — On-farm experimentation, robotic interseeding
- **Ginny Moore & Erika Everest** — Dry-bean presentation and tasting (tasting over lunch)
- **Quirine Ketterings & Kirsten Workman** — Value of manure and SSEA approach
- **Lilly Elliott & Katja Poveda** — Seedcorn maggot: predicting damage risk in New York
- **Kristen Loria & Matt Ryan** — Balancing trade-offs when no-till planting into cover crops
- **Joseph Amsili** — Soil health benchmarks, carbon stocks, and solar farms

For more information visit [cals.cornell.edu/2024-aurora-farm-field-day](https://cals.cornell.edu/2024-aurora-farm-field-day) or contact Louis Longchamps at [ll928@cornell.edu](mailto:ll928@cornell.edu).
