![Soil Health Field Days](/images/news/Screenshot-2024-06-12-at-8.47.47 AM.png#left) The NY Soil Health team held 11 soil health and climate resiliency field days across New York State during June through September 2024. These field days were designed to help farmers by providing practical information and demonstrations.

Field days covered a variety of topics including vineyard soil health, soil biology, urban soil health, biochar, cover crops, orchard soil health, organic field crops, and pasture and silvopasture soil health.

For more information about NY Soil Health and upcoming field days, visit newyorksoilhealth.org.
