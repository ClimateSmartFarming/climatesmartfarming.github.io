![PASA Conference logo](/images/news/PASA.png#left) The Climate Smart Farming team presented at PASA's (Pennsylvania Action for Sustainable Agriculture) 25th Annual Farming for the Future Conference in State College, PA.

The 2016 conference ran from February 3rd to 6th at the Penn Stater Conference Center. This is the largest sustainable agriculture conference in the East, convening over 2,000 farmers, processors, consumers, students, environmentalists, and business and community leaders every year. The theme of "Farming in a Changing Climate" reflected the ever-increasing impact of climate change on agricultural systems, and the need to raise awareness of best management practices for mitigation and adaptation.

The CSF team was well represented at the conference. Art DeGaetano, Director of Cornell's Northeast Regional Climate Center, gave a workshop with Dan Dostie from the USDA-NRCS Climate Hub entitled "Northeast Climate Change Impact Projections & Planning Considerations for Selecting Adaptation Practices."

Allison Chatrchyan and Mike Hoffmann, CSF Program Founders and Director and Executive Director of the Cornell Institute for Climate Change, presented a workshop entitled "Impacts, Opportunities & Adaptations: Climate-Smart Farming in the Northeast."
