Land, Economy, Agriculture, and Forestry… and AI? In July 2026, Cornell's AI-LEAF team introduced New York 4-H'ers to these critical topics. Middle and high school students from across New York state spent three days discovering potential career paths and came to Cornell to participate in the annual [4-H Career Explorations Conference](https://www.nys4-h.org/career-explorations). In the AI-LEAF session, youth had the opportunity to interact with digital agriculture tools that utilize AI to help farmers make decisions given environmental uncertainty. By exploring the Cornell Climate Smart Farming and the Digital Soil Twin tools, 4-H'ers were able to see how AI is shaping the future in a positive way, more than just how ChatGPT can help them with their homework. The session was led by Candace Hulbert, Cornell Soil and Crop Sciences; a Cornell postdoctoral student, Jacob Boes; and two undergraduate students, Quincy Hansen and Willow Martin, which gave them an invaluable opportunity to practice translating scientific research for varied audiences.

To read more, see the article in the [Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/07/cornell-4-hers-plant-seeds-future-careers).
