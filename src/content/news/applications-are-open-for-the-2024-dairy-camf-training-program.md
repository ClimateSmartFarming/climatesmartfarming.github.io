![Dairy cows at Cornell](/images/news/cow-cornell-image.jpeg#left) The Dairy Climate Adaptation Fellowship program began in Winter 2024, offering a cohort-based learning opportunity for farmers and agricultural advisors in the Northeast interested in climate change adaptation and mitigation strategies, planning, and peer-to-peer networking and support.

Dairy farmers and agricultural advisors who work with dairy farmers in New York, Vermont, and Maine were eligible to apply for this program.

For more information about the Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Fellowship program, visit adaptationfellows.net.
