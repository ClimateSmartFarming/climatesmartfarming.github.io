![Climate Smart Farming booth at Empire Farm Days](/images/news/EFD.jpg#right) Each year, the Cornell Climate Smart Farming Team participates in the annual Empire Farm Days in Seneca Falls, NY. The 2016 event was held from August 9th to 11th and was attended by farmers, extension professionals, and gardeners from New York State and beyond.

The CSF Team displayed its new CSF Decision Tools on an interactive, touch-screen computer. Farmers were able to enter their farm's location and see outputs on tools such as the Growing Degree Day Calculator, Irrigation/Water Deficit Calculator, and Frost/Freeze Risk Tool, which can help them make informed, climate-smart decisions.
