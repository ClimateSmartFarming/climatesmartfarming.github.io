![FAO Digital Agriculture event](/images/news/FAO.jpg#right) Cornell faculty and students participated in "Travel Around the World with Digital FAO and Agro-Informatics," a virtual workshop series hosted by the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations on September 10, 2025.

The lineup of Cornell presenters included Allison Chatrchyan, Ph.D. (Climate-Smart Farming Tools & AI-LEAF Institute), Louis Longchamps, Ph.D. (Digital Agriculture & On-Farm Experimentation), Phillips Lanza (CALS Ph.D. Student), Lee Dunnigan (Cornell & AI-LEAF Ph.D. Student), and Haodi Xu (Cornell & AI-LEAF Ph.D. Student).

The session featured presentations on climate-smart farming innovations and digital agriculture tools, followed by a Q&A and discussion with participants from around the world.
