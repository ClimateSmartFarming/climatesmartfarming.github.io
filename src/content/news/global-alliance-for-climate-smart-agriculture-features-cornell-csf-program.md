![GACSA Newsletter](/images/news/Screen Shot 2017-02-27 at 1.54.02 PM.png#left) The Cornell Climate Smart Farming Program was featured in the Global Alliance for Climate Smart Agriculture's (GACSA) February 2017 Newsletter.

CICSS Director and spearheader of the Cornell CSF Program, Allison Chatrchyan, is actively involved with GACSA, participating in their annual meetings each year and developing case studies of climate smart agriculture from the Northeast to contribute to their worldwide assessments.
