Over the winter to early spring of 2025, members of Cornell's Climate Smart Farming team — **Allison Chatrchyan**, **Yolanda Gonzalez**, and **Savanna Shelnutt** — began working with the USDA Northeast Climate Hub (**Kristin Benson**) to run an Urban Climate Adaptation & Mitigation Program in New York City.

The Urban Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Program (Urban CAMP) is a peer-to-peer-based learning and training opportunity for urban farmers and technical service providers who are interested in climate change adaptation and mitigation. The program serves urban producers who are thinking about climate change, are interested in learning about climate impacts, and are motivated to pursue climate adaptation and/or mitigation on their farms in NYC and the Metropolitan area. It also supports service providers working with urban farmers who want to strengthen their ability to provide climate-focused guidance.

Over 60 urban farmers and advisors completed the five-week, in-person training. A subset of the group are now working to develop ten urban farm adaptation or mitigation plans for farms they work with.

Urban CAMP is a partnership between Cornell's Climate Smart Farming Program, Cornell Cooperative Extension's Harvest NY Program, and the USDA Northeast Climate Hub. The curriculum draws heavily from the Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Fellowship (CAMF), which was developed in response to farmers' need for information and technical assistance related to climate change.

## Program Goals

Urban CAMP is designed to build lasting climate leadership in New York City's agricultural community. The program aims to provide geographically specific climate change information relevant to urban farmers — primarily vegetable and small fruit production, including cut flowers, herbs, mushrooms, and value-added products. It also works to empower urban farmers and agricultural advisors to become climate leaders in their communities through peer-to-peer learning, and to create opportunities for farmers to develop farm-specific climate adaptation and mitigation plans with support. Strengthening agricultural advisor knowledge in climate planning and supporting the development of outreach products that reach farmers beyond the program are also central goals.

## How the Program Works

The program consists of five in-person workshops, additional online expert speakers, an independent work period with regularly scheduled check-ins, and a closing workshop. The in-person workshops consist of five two-hour sessions, followed by field trips to farms over the summer. The independent work period runs from March through December 2025, with regular check-ins with the CSF and Harvest NY teams. The closing workshop will take place in December 2025.

## 2025 Urban CAMP Sessions

In February and March 2025, 62 urban agricultural stakeholders participated in five workshop sessions covering the full arc of climate-smart farming — from understanding the science to securing funding for on-farm projects.

|---|---|---|
| Session 1 | Feb 26 | Climate Science and Impacts |
| Session 2 | March 5 | Climate Adaptation |
| Session 3 | March 12 | Climate Mitigation |
| Session 4 | March 19 | Climate Planning |
| Session 5 | March 26 | Climate Funding |
| Final Session | Winter 2025 | Program Recognition and Sharing |
