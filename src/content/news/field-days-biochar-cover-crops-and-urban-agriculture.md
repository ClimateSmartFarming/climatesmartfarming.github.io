![New York Soil Health Field Days](/images/news/Screenshot-2025-09-04-at-4.40.36 PM.png#left) The New York Soil Health Field Days event on September 9, 2025 featured presentations on biochar, cover crops, urban agriculture, and more.

The event took place from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. at Spruce Haven Farm in Union Springs, NY. Attendees had the opportunity to learn about biochar and its application on farms from expert presenters.

For more information about New York Soil Health initiatives and upcoming events, visit fielddays.newyorksoilhealth.org.
