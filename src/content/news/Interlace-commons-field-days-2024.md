Interlace Commons's mission is to promote the adoption of agroforestry land-use practices that address climate change and land degradation, moving towards resilient, biodiverse landscapes that enhance livelihoods. Their mission is achieved through farmer-driven field projects, education, and research initiatives throughout the Northeastern United States.

Interlace Commons will be holding free field day events across the Northeast this summer and fall. Visit the link below to learn more and register!
