In September, Cornell faculty and students from Climate Smart Farming and AI-LEAF joined the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations for a virtual workshop titled "Travel Around the World with Digital FAO and Agro-Informatics."

The session featured **Dr. Allison Chatrchyan**, **Dr. Louis Longchamps**, and Ph.D. students **Phillip Lanza**, **Lee Dunnigan**, and **Haodi Xu**, who presented their work on using artificial intelligence to help farmers mitigate and adapt to climate change.

![AI-LEAF researchers discussing digital agriculture tools for the UN FAO series](/images/news/fao-watch-2.jpg)

The virtual workshop is part of a series that connects universities from around the world to showcase how researchers can use digital tools in the study of agrifood systems.
