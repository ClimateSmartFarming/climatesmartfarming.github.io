![Grape shoot freeze damage](/images/news/grape-shoot-freeze_5-27-14.jpg#left) The CICSS Team showcased an updated and improved Version 2.0 of the CSF Grape Hardiness and Freeze Risk Tool at the 2018 NYS Ag Society Forum on January 4th.

The new version of the tool included updated weather and forecast data and a faster graphical output to improve the user experience. The Northeast was experiencing an extreme cold snap at the time, and the tool was predicting temperatures that could damage grapes and other perennial crops in parts of the Northeast region. Armed with more precise forecast information related to their crops, farmers can implement mitigative practices to reduce crop losses.

Dr. Toby Ault also presented a talk at the CALS Alumni reception on the topic of "Data Driven Decision Making," featuring his work with the CSF Program and tools.
