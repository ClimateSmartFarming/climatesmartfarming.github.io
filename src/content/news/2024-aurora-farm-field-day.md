![Musgrave Research Farm aerial view](/images/news/musgrave-field-day-aerial.jpg#right) The annual Cornell Research Field Day was held August 1st, 2024 at Musgrave Research Farm in Aurora, NY. The event included walking and hay wagon tours with multiple tour stops and speakers.

Presentations covered a wide range of topics including herbicide programs for weed control in corn and soybean (Vipan Kumar), weed seed movement and the importance of cleaning equipment (Mike Hunter & Lynn Sosnoskie), on-farm experimentation and robotic interseeding (Louis Longchamps), dry-bean presentation and tasting (Ginny Moore and Erika Everest), value of manure and the SSEA approach (Quirine Ketterings and Kirsten Workman), seedcorn maggot damage risk prediction in New York (Lilly Elliott & Katja Poveda), balancing trade-offs when no-till planting into cover crops (Kristen Loria and Matt Ryan), and soil health benchmarks, carbon stocks, and solar farms (Joseph Amsili).

For more information about future Aurora Farm Field Day events, contact Louis Longchamps at ll928@cornell.edu.
