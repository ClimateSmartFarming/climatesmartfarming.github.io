![Urban Ag Climate Field Days](/images/news/image003.png#left) Cornell Cooperative Extension's Ag Climate Resiliency Program, Cornell Climate Smart Farming and Cornell Soil Health, and Harvest NY hosted field days in July 2024 focused on the basics of climate resilience, adaptation planning, and soil health.

The events were designed for anyone interested in learning about best practices for building climate resilience on urban farms, from seasoned farmers to those just getting started.

Two field day sessions were held: July 11th, 2024 at Brooklyn Grange Navy Yard and July 12th, 2024 at Governor's Island Teaching Garden.
