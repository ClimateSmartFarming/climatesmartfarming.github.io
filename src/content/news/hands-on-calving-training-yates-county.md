# Hands-On Calving Training - Yates County

Join the Hands-On Calving Training - Yates County held by Cornell Cooperative Extension Northwest New York Dairy, Livestock & Field Crops to learn best calving management practices. This is an in-person workshop that includes a presentation and hands-on training. Performance objects of this event include…
Performance Objectives: 

- Understand Anatomy and Physiology of Calving
- Recognize and Monitor Signs of Labor
- Assess Normal and Abnormal Calf Position
- Properly Assist the Calving Process
- Use Chains, Pulley/Calf-Jack Safely and Effectively
- Immediate Post-Partum Cow and Calf Care

Hands-On Calving Training - Yates County will be held August 7th, 2024 from 1pm-4pm at the Skyline Auction Center Benton, NY. The Cost is $40 per person and attendance is limited. For more information and to register visit <https://nwnyteam.cce.cornell.edu/event.php?id=2417>
