# Latest Drought Newsletter from the Emergent Climate Risk Lab

Read the second [Drought Newsletter](http://ecrl.eas.cornell.edu/Misc/NEDrought/ECRL-CICCS-NEDroughtNewsletter.vol1.3.pdf) from Cornell's Emergent Climate Risk Lab (ECRL) headed up by Dr. Toby R. Ault.  This is the second issue in this newsletter series and highlights include an overview of current drought conditions in the Northeastern United States, predicted changes in drought conditions and an introduction to the CICSS and ECRL newly launched [drought visualization tool](http://climatesmartfarming.org/tools/csf-nys-ne-drought-atlas/).
