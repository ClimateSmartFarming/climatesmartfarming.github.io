The New York Department of Agriculture and Markets has announced a funding opportunity for New York State Soil and Water Conservation Districts to complete projects that mitigate agriculture's impact on climate change. The funds can also be used for projects that enhance on-farm adaptation and resiliency to increased climate change effects.

## Eligible Project Tracks

Applications must fall into one of the following categories:

- **Track 1 — Livestock Management:** Alternative Waste Management and Precision Feed Management
- **Track 2 — Adaptation and Resiliency**
- **Track 3 — Healthy Soils NY:** Systems and BMPs that support soil health and agroforestry
- **Track 4 — Agricultural Forest Management**


## How to Apply

The deadline for submitting proposals is **5:00 pm local time on November 17, 2025**. Proposals must be submitted by a New York State Soil and Water Conservation District on the State Committee's SharePoint site to be considered for funding.

For more information and to apply, visit the [NYS Climate-Resilient Farming Program RFP page](https://agriculture.ny.gov/rfp-0389-climate-resilient-farming-program-round-9).
