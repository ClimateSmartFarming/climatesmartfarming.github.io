![Climate Central article screenshot](/images/news/climate-central-interview.png#right) Cornell Institute for Climate Smart Solutions Directors Mike Hoffmann and Allison Chatrchyan were interviewed by Climate Central about perspectives on climate change and agricultural policy.

The interview focused on how transitions in government leadership could impact agencies such as the USDA and the work being done in the climate change and agricultural sphere. Hoffmann and Chatrchyan shared their perspectives on the direction of these organizations and the importance of continued support for climate-smart agriculture initiatives.
