![Climate Smart Farming Exhibit poster](/images/news/CICCA-Exhibit_May-5th-Event.png#left) The Cornell Institute for Climate Change and Agriculture hosted a multimedia exhibit at Mann Library on Cornell's campus, featuring photos and video footage from the Climate Smart Farming program.

The opening reception on May 5, 2016 featured Dale Stein of Stein Farms, Paul King of Six Mile Creek Vineyards, Glenn Evans of the Cornell Agricultural Experiment Station, and Allison Chatrchyan of CICCA.

The CSF program works with Cornell researchers, extension specialists, and New York State farmers to co-develop tools that increase resiliency, reduce risk, and mitigate greenhouse gas emissions from the agricultural sector. This exhibit showcased this unique collaboration, highlighting stories of farmers' experiences with extreme weather and climate change and the important adaptations they are making through new cropping systems and varieties, improved water and waste management, and the installation of renewable energy systems.

The exhibit ran from May through August 2016 as part of Mann Library's year-long special programming series on climate change. A second event featuring a special talk with Dr. Toby Ault was held on June 10, 2016.
