Join Cornell Cooperative Extension's Ag Climate Resiliency Program, Cornell Climate Smart Farming, Cornell Soil Health, and Harvest NY to learn all about the basics of climate resilience, adaptation planning, and soil health. Whether you're a seasoned farmer or just getting started, this event is perfect for anyone interested in building climate resilience on urban farms. Light refreshments will be served.

**Please note:** These are two separate events — please choose the date that works best for your schedule.
