![Interlace Commons Field Days](/images/news/Screenshot-2024-08-09-at-4.07.01 PM.png#right) Interlace Commons's mission is to promote the adoption of agroforestry land-use practices that address climate change and land degradation and move towards resilient, biodiverse landscapes that enhance livelihoods.

Their mission is achieved through farmer-driven field projects, education, and research initiatives throughout the Northeastern United States.

Interlace Commons held free field day events in the Northeast during summer and fall 2024. For more information about Interlace Commons and their upcoming events, visit their website.
