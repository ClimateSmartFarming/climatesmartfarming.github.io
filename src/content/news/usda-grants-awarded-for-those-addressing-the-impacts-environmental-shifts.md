![USDA logo](/images/news/USDA-logo.jpg#left) The United States Department of Agriculture's National Institute of Food and Agriculture (NIFA) announced nine grants totaling over $8 million dollars in July 2017. These grants were awarded to research centers focused on the study and development of new approaches to the agricultural sector.

Among the awardees was the Cornell Institute for Climate Smart Solutions Climate Smart Farming program.
