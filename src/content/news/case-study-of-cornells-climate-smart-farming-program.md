![CSF Case Study](/images/news/Screen-Shot-2017-07-18-at-2.40.35-PM.png#right) Together with the Global Alliance for Climate-Smart Agriculture (GACSA), Cornell's Climate Smart Farming Program created a case study highlighting the resources, training, and decision support tools for farmers in the Northeastern United States.

The case study explores how the research and extension outreach program provides support tools for farmers in the Northeast. The program is built on trusted two-way feedback between researchers, extension staff, and farmers.

The case study is available through the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations.
