![Flooded Schoharie farm](/images/news/floodedschohariefarm_2011_4_lg_nrcsny_2.jpg#right) The Cornell University Climate Change Seminar featured a series of weekly talks related to the connection between climate change and agriculture in spring 2016.

The agriculture-related talks included Christine Goodale discussing "Climate Change and Terrestrial Ecosystems," David Wolfe speaking about "Climate Change and the Future of Food," and Linda Mearns presenting "A Look at the Evolution of Determining the Impacts of Climate Change through the IPCC."

The Cornell Institute for Climate Change and Agriculture (CICCA) and the David R. Atkinson Center for a Sustainable Future (ACSF) created this seminar series, which focused on "Perspectives on the Climate Change Challenge." The seminar gave students and other attendees the opportunity to hear climate change experts speak on their research, perspectives, and recommendations regarding the challenges that climate change poses.

Additional talks in the series included Katherin McComas discussing "Climate Change Communication" and Ravi Kanbur talking about "Climate Justice, Economics, and Philosophy."
