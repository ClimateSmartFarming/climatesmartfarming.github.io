![Climate Smart Farming](/images/news/DSC00053-e1457449102426.jpg#right) The Cornell Chronicle reported on a case study examining Cornell's Climate Smart Farming program. The article highlighted the UN's overall effort to examine regional programs that support farmers in adapting to climate change.

Programs such as the Climate Smart Farming program support the UN's goal to ensure global food security by providing farmers with decision support tools and resources to build resilience.
