The Grape Hardiness Tool helps vineyard managers monitor cold hardiness levels and assess winter freeze risk.

## How do I use this tool?

View our video tool tutorial on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/9rHmYJ5MFTU).

## Instructions

### Choosing Your Location
- Click the "Change Location" button to select your vineyard location
- Select your grape variety to see hardiness thresholds
