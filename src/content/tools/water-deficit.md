The Water Deficit Calculator helps farmers monitor soil moisture and plan irrigation based on weather data and crop water requirements.

## How do I use this tool?

View our video tool tutorial on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/kt0FN4k7JXk).

## Instructions

### Choosing Your Location
- Click the "Change Location" button to select your farm location
- Enter your address, zip code, or click on the map

### Setting Soil and Crop Type
- Select your soil type and crop to get accurate water deficit calculations
