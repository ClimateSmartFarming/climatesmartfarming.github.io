NOAA's seasonal precipitation outlook provides 1-3 month precipitation predictions for the United States.

## About This Tool

The Climate Prediction Center issues official U.S. seasonal outlooks for precipitation. These maps show the probability of above-normal, near-normal, or below-normal precipitation for the upcoming months.

## How to Use

Visit the NOAA Climate Prediction Center website to view current seasonal outlooks and explore historical data.
