The Growing Degree Day Calculator helps farmers track heat accumulation for crops and insects using customizable base temperatures.

## How do I use this tool?

View our video tool tutorial on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/L7dVBuHLFrQ).

## Instructions

### Choosing Your Location
- Click the "Change Location" button to select your farm location
- Enter your address, zip code, or click on the map

### Setting Base Temperature
- Select from preset crops/insects or enter a custom base temperature
- The tool calculates GDD accumulation from your selected start date
