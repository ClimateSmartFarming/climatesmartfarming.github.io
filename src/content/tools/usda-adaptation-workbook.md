The Adaptation Workbook provides a structured process for climate adaptation planning.

## About This Tool

The Adaptation Workbook is a flexible process for integrating climate change considerations into land management and conservation. It helps natural resource managers:

- Assess climate change impacts
- Evaluate management goals
- Develop adaptation strategies
- Implement and monitor actions

## How to Use

Visit the Adaptation Workbook website to access the online tool and supporting resources.
