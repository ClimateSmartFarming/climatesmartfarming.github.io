The Cover Crop Planner helps farmers determine optimal seeding dates for cover crops based on local climate conditions.

## How do I use this tool?

This tool helps you plan cover crop seeding by analyzing historical climate data for your location.

## Instructions

### Choosing Your Location
- Click the "Change Location" button to select your farm location
- Select your cover crop species to see recommended seeding windows
