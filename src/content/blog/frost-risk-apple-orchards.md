Spring frosts after bloom can devastate an apple crop in a single night. Knowing when to act — and when not to — is the difference between a full crop and a write-off.

## The Frapple Tool

The CSF Apple Stage / Freeze Damage Probability Tool (Frapple) tracks phenological stage based on accumulated growing degree days and gives you a probability of damage given the forecast low temperature.

## Decision Thresholds

At green tip, temperatures need to drop to 15°F before significant damage occurs. By pink stage, damage can occur at 28°F. Full bloom is the most sensitive period — 28°F for 30 minutes can cause 10% kill.

## Running Protection

Wind machines are most effective when there is a temperature inversion — typically on clear, calm nights. Use the frost probability forecast to decide when the risk justifies the fuel cost.
