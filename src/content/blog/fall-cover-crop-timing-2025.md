Fall cover crop establishment is one of the highest-leverage decisions a Northeast farmer can make. Get the timing wrong and you lose the benefits of winter cover entirely.

## Use the Scheduler

The CSF Cover Crop Planting Date Scheduler uses your zip code and local climate data to recommend a seeding window. For most of New York, that window falls between August 15 and September 15 for small grains.

## Species Considerations

Winter rye is the most reliable option for late plantings. Hairy vetch provides excellent nitrogen but needs more time to establish — plan accordingly.

## What to Watch

Keep an eye on GDD accumulation after seeding. Adequate heat unit accumulation before a killing frost is essential for winter survival of legume species.
