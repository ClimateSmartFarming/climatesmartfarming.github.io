As we head into the 2025 growing season, climate variability continues to present both challenges and opportunities for Northeast farmers. This post highlights three key areas to focus on this year.

## 1. Extended Shoulder Seasons

Growing degree day accumulations have been running above average in recent years. Use the CSF GDD Calculator to track heat accumulation and adjust planting windows accordingly.

## 2. Precipitation Variability

Expect continued swings between wet and dry periods. Investing in soil health — through cover crops and reduced tillage — is one of the most reliable buffers against both drought and flooding.

## 3. Frost Risk

Later last frosts and earlier first frosts are shifting. Review your frost risk data using the CSF Frost & Freeze Risk Tool before making decisions on early transplants or late-season crops.
