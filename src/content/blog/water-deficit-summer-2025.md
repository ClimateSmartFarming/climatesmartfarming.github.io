July and August often bring the most stressful water conditions for Northeast vegetable and field crop farmers. Here's how to get the most out of the CSF Water Deficit Calculator during a dry stretch.

## What the Tool Shows

The Water Deficit Calculator estimates how much water your crop has used relative to what's been available from rainfall and irrigation. When the bar turns red, your crop is likely experiencing stress.

## What to Do When You See Stress

Don't wait for wilting. Once the tool shows moderate deficit, schedule irrigation within 24–48 hours for shallow-rooted crops like lettuce and onions.

## Calibrating for Your Soil

Make sure you've entered the correct soil type. Sandy soils hold far less water than loams — selecting the wrong type will give you inaccurate readings.
