Candace Hulbert is the Program Manager for the NSF/NIFA-funded AI-LEAF Institute (AI for Land, Economy, Agriculture & Forestry), and the Cornell Climate Smart Farming program. Candace's work with AI-LEAF centers around public engagement, stakeholder education, and extension related to Climate-Smart Agriculture, digital agriculture, and artificial intelligence in agriculture.

Candace received her MPS in Natural Resources and the Environment from Cornell University in 2023 and her BA in English and Political Science from the University of Michigan in 2020. From January 2021 to August 2023, Candace coordinated the Weather Ready Farms program at the University of Nebraska Extension.
