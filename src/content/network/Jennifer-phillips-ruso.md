Jennifer brings local experience and research-based solutions together to provide projects aimed at increasing yields, product quality, diversity and improvement of cultivars, the efficiency of production, profitability, and adoption of environmentally sound cultural management strategies with an emphasis on soil and grapevine health, site selection, growth control, nutrition, and water management, and harvest management. She holds her B.S. and M.S. degrees in Biology from The State University of New York at Fredonia.
