Janice is the Team Leader and Field Crops Specialist with the SCNY Dairy & Field Crops Team, based in Cortland, NY. Janice has agriculture in her blood, having spent summers on her grandmother's dairy farm as a child. As a worldwide traveler who has explored interests in international agriculture and is fluent in Spanish, she can provide communication assistance to farmers. Janice works with farmers to help them with crop production issues, integrated crop management, environmental stewardship, soil health, and forage quality. She holds an M.P.S. in plant protection and a B.S. in agronomy, both from Cornell University. She worked on the Dairy Climate Adaptation Fellowship Program, training dairy farmers and advisors about climate resiliency.
