Deborah Aller is a Senior Extension Associate and Executive Director of the New York Center for Organics Recycling and Education (NYCORE) in the School of Integrative Plant Science, Soil and Crop Sciences Section at Cornell University.

Her work focuses on soil health, organics management and biochar, and agricultural stewardship.
