Kristin Benson is an ORISE Fellow with the USDA Forest Service and the USDA Northeast Climate Hub, where she works to support farmer and landowner economic and environmental sustainability in the Northeast. Kristin grew up in a dairy farming family in Gorham, Maine and became passionate about agricultural sustainability early on.

She received her Bachelor's degree in Environmental Science from the University of Southern Maine and her Master's in International Agriculture and Rural Development from Cornell University. She has a background in curriculum development and agricultural sustainability and is supporting farmers as they transition to climate-smart practices. Kristin has been co-leading the Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Programs with Cornell, working with dairy and urban farmers.
