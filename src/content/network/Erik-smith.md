Dr. Erik Smith works as a Field Crops Specialist with the Cornell Cooperative Extension Central New York Dairy, Livestock & Field Crops Team. Erik has 15 years of experience conducting agricultural research and educational outreach as a graduate student and Extension professional. As a Finger Lakes native with grain and forage crop experience gained on his family's farm, he brings his knowledge to New York's agricultural community.

Erik earned his B.A. from SUNY Oswego in Biology and his M.S. and Ph.D. in Entomology from Cornell University.
