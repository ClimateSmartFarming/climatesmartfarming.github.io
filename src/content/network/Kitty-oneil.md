Dr. Kitty O'Neil works to improve the yield and production efficiency of field crops and forages — a goal that requires smart crop management and a keen understanding of climate-related risks and long-term soil health. A field crops and soils specialist, she leads the North New York field crops team, which designs crop and soil management programs to serve the region's farms. Kitty's research has included cropping systems and the effects of soil amendments and cover crops on soil health on potato farms.

She earned a B.S. in animal science from Cornell University, an M.S. in animal nutrition and plant biochemistry, and a Ph.D. in sustainable plant and soil cropping systems, both from Michigan State University. Kitty worked on the Dairy Climate Adaptation Fellowship Program, training dairy farmers and advisors about climate resiliency. She Co-Chairs the Cornell Extension Climate Change and Agriculture Program Work Team.
