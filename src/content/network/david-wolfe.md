David Wolfe is Emeritus Professor of Plant and Soil Ecology in the School of Integrative Plant Science (Horticulture Section) at Cornell University. His primary research interests and expertise include soil and water conservation and management, plant stress physiology, and new tools for climate change adaptation and mitigation for managed and natural ecosystems.
