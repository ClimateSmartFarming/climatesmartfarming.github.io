The New York State Climate Resilient Farming (CRF) Grant Program provides cost‐share assistance to farmers to reduce GHG emissions and increase resiliency through one of three project categories: 1) agricultural waste storage cover and flare systems for methane reduction; 2) on-farm water management; and 3) soil health systems. The program is a competitive grant program, with funds applied for and awarded through county Soil and Water Conservation Districts on behalf of farmers. State funds come from the New York State Environmental Protection Fund. For more information: contact:
