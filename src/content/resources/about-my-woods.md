[About My Woods](https://www.nefainfo.org/aboutmywoods.html) is a **free** smartphone app, is now available for download.  Woodland owners in Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont and New York now have a new tool to help learn about their woods.

Available for both **Android** and **iPhones** (and tablets), you can access the app by going to the App Store (Apple) or Play Store (Android) and simply typing “About My Woods” in the search bar. 

**Developer:** North East State Foresters Association
**Cost:** Free
**Time Investment:** Low
