Developed by the University of Aberdeen in conjunction with Unilever, the [Cool Farm Tool](https://coolfarm.org/) is an online greenhouse gas calculator that allows farmers to measure and better understand emissions from their crops and livestock. The tool is designed to be intuitive and easy to use with information that a farmer will have readily available. In addition to calculating on farm GHG emissions, the tool allows users to test alternative management practices and identify emission hot spots. 

**Developer:** University of Aberdeen, Unilever
Cost: Free
Time Investment: High
