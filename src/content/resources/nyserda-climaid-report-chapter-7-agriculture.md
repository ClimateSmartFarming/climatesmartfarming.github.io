The New York ClimAID report was published in 2011 through a partnership between NYSERDA, Columbia University, The City University of New York, and Cornell University. The report is a comprehensive assessment of current and projected climate change vulnerabilities relevant to New York State, chapter 7 pertains specifically to agriculture in New York State.
