## Introduction to the Workbook

The Climate Adaptation Workbook is a practical guide to understanding climate change impacts, Climate Informed Farming, and climate change adaptation strategies. This workbook includes worksheets to help farmers assess risks, choose solutions, and implement changes to improve their farm's resilience.

## The Climate Adaptation Workbook

### Climate Challenges for Farmers

The most severe impacts of climate change in the Northeast include extreme precipitation, short-term drought, heat stress, warmer winters, late-spring freezes, increased pest pressure, and higher production costs—impacts that will intensify as temperature and precipitation extremes continue to grow.

### Climate Smart Farming and Risk Management

A Climate Smart Farming approach recognizes changing conditions and helps farmers evaluate and experiment with different strategies, practices, and management decisions to protect crops and livestock, improve farm resiliency, reduce greenhouse gas emissions, and ensure sustainable farm incomes and food security. Agricultural adaptation is highly specific and depends on farm location and size, observed climate impacts, commodities produced, and costs. Climate Smart Farm planning helps producers prioritize their farm's long-term sustainability and serve as stewards of their land.
