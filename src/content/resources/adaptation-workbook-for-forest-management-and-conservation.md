The adaptation workbook, created by the Northern Institute of Applied Climate Science, is targeted toward forest managers and natural resource professionals. The workbook consists of a flexible five step process designed to help resource managers consider the potential effects of climate change on forest ecosystems at a variety of scales and locations. This approach enables users to plan potential adaptation actions beneficial to their specific needs.
