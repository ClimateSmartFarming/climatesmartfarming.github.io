[PRO-DAIRY](http://prodairy.cals.cornell.edu/about-us) links New York’s dairy farmers and agribusiness professionals to critical research and resources, giving them the information they need to build and manage robust businesses.

PRO-DAIRY’s contributions to educational programming and applied research help New York farmers continue to lead in milk production and remain among the top dairy states.  Through its research, discussion groups, educational programs, financial analysis, websites, publications and personal contacts, the program reaches thousands of dairy professionals annually.
