The [Climate Vulnerabilities in the Northeast](http://climatesmartfarming.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Northeast-VA-Summary-2016.pdf) factsheet outlines the main "Climate-Related Hazards and Vulnerabilities," and gives a broad overview of "Regional Priorities" and "Adaptation and Mitigation Strategies."
