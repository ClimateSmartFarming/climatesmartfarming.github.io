It is projected that pressure from invasive species and other pests will change with the onset of climate change. The New York State Integrated Pest Management Program (IPM) is part of Cornell Cooperative Extension, providing resources and services for addressing pest management needs.  IPM helps farmers and land managers deal with pests ranging from insects, plant diseases, weeds, and more while working to keep health and environmental risks as low as possible.
