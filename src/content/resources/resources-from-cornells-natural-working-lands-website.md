Cornell's Natural and Working Lands website provides tools, training webinars, videos, factsheets, and other resources to help manage land resources to address climate change, improve air and water, and support vibrant communities. The program is dedicated to helping farmers, forest owners, and landowners identify meaningful and pragmatic ways to reduce greenhouse gases (GHG) from the land. 

Contact [Jenifer Wightman](mailto:jenifer.wightman@cornell.edu) and [Peter Woodbury](mailto:peterwoodbury@cornell.edu) for more details.
