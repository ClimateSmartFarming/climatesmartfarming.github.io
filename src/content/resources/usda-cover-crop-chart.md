The Cover Crop Chart is produced and distributed by staff of the USDA-ARS. It is an easy to use pdf file that guides users on cover crop selection. The chart includes 58 crop species that may be planted individually or in conjunction with one another. Information about each specie’s growth cycle, water use, plant architecture, seeding depth, forage quality, pollination characteristics, and nutrient cycling is provided by clicking on the links provided in the chart itself.
