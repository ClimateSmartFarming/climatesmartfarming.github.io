**Implications of Large Solar Installations and Leasing on Farmland
Zoom Webinar: ****Friday, February 12, 2021 **

Utility-scale solar projects have been expanding in NY recently due to the need for additional energy development, changing technology, and changes in policies at the state level. This webinar will discuss some of the implications of solar siting on farmland and how we can support renewable energy development while protecting farmland.
