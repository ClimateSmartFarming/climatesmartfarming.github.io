Published by Rutgers Climate Institute, this [report](http://climatechange.rutgers.edu/docman-list/affiliate-publications/449-ag-and-climate-document-final-1/file) examines the potential impacts of climate change on agriculture, aquaculture and fisheries throughout New Jersey.  This brief fact sheet is intended to provide an overview of the various impacts of climate change and provide potential adaptation strategies for New Jersey farmers and fishers.

You can also explore their other reports on climate change and agriculture in New Jersey, as well as videos [here](http://climatechange.rutgers.edu/resources/climate-change-and-agriculture).
