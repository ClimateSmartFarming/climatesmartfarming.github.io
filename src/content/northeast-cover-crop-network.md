## Project Overview

The Northeast Cover Crop Network is a collaborative research initiative spanning six states, evaluating how different cover crop species and mixes perform under varying soil types, climate conditions, and farming systems.

## Objectives

- Evaluate cover crop winter survival and biomass production across the Northeast
- Assess impacts on cash crop yields and soil health indicators
- Develop region-specific recommendations for farmers

## Partners

Cornell University, University of Vermont, Penn State Extension, University of Maine

## Funding

USDA Sustainable Agriculture Research & Education (SARE) Program

## Status

Active — data collection ongoing through 2026.
