The Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Fellowship (CAMF) is a peer-to-peer learning program that builds climate resilience across farms, forests, and communities. CAMF is a partnership between the USDA Northeast Climate Hub and Cornell University, designed to empower farmers and agricultural advisors to gain and share knowledge and tools needed to be resilient in the face of climate uncertainty.
In 2025, Allison Chatrchyan, Yolanda Gonzalez and Savanna Shelnutt are training urban farmers and advisors in New York City, with the CCE Harvest NY Program, and the USDA NE Climate Hub (Kristin Benson).
In 2024, members of the Cornell Climate Smart Farming team facilitated the Climate Adaptation Fellowship Program for dairy farmers and advisors in New York, Vermont, and Maine. The program trained dairy producers and advisors who were concerned about climate impacts on farms, and were motivated to develop climate adaptation and/or mitigation plans for their operations. The program also helped train agriculture service providers to work with dairy farmers on planning and implementing strategies, and strengthened their ability to provide climate-focused support.
