Three years ago, we were struggling with compaction on our lower fields after a particularly wet fall. A conversation with our Cornell Cooperative Extension agent led us to try a winter rye and crimson clover mix as a cover crop.

## What We Did

We used the CSF Cover Crop Planting Date Scheduler to identify the optimal seeding window for our location in Tompkins County. We seeded approximately 85 acres in mid-September.

## What We Saw

By the following spring, we noticed significantly better water infiltration in the previously compacted areas. Soil organic matter increased from 2.1% to 3.4% over three seasons.

## The Bottom Line

Input costs dropped by roughly 12% as we needed less fertilizer in fields with established cover crop rotations. We're now expanding the practice to all of our tillable acres.
